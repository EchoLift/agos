## Current Status

Agency settings has a dedicated owner-only route, but the settings surface is still lightweight.

## Who Can Use It

Owners can access agency settings. Non-owner roles should not see or use agency settings.

## Planned Direction

Agency settings should eventually include workspace identity, billing, role customization, and operational defaults.
