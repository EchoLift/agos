## When To Use It

Use multiple roles when one person writes and edits, shoots and edits, or manages and reviews.

## Example

EchoLift can be both Writer and Editor. They can receive writing work and editing work when assigned.

## What It Does Not Mean

Having a role does not automatically assign all matching work. The person still needs to be the task owner, gig assignee, reviewer, or relevant campaign participant.
