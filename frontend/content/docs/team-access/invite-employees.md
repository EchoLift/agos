## How Invitations Work

You invite someone by email and assign roles. When they login with that email, AGOS connects the invitation to their user account.

## Why It Matters

The employee can use one login across multiple agencies. Their role can be different in each agency.

## What Happens Next

After acceptance, the employee appears in Team and can receive workflow tasks or gigs.
