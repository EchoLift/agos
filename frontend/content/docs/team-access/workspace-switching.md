## How To Switch

Open the profile menu and choose another workspace under Switch Workspace.

## What Happens Next

AGENCIE validates that you are a member of that agency and updates the active workspace.

## What Can Go Wrong

If you cannot see a workspace, make sure the invitation email matches the email you used to log in.

> [!IMPORTANT] Tenant Safety
> Agency data is isolated. You only see the clients, campaigns, gigs, and workflow for the active workspace.
