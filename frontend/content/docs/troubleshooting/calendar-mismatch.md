## Why It Happens

My Work is an assignment queue. Calendar is a time view.

A work item may appear in Workflow but not Calendar if it has no deadline. A calendar item may appear because it is a publishing slot, not because it is assigned to you.

## What To Do

Check scope, visible calendars, assignee, owner, reviewer, and deadline.

Use My Schedule when you want only your work. Use Agency scope only if you have permission to see the broader agency calendar.
