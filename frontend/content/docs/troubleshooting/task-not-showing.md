## Common Cause

Having a role is not the same as being assigned.

If you are an editor, you only see editor work in My Schedule when the task is assigned to you or the calendar scope includes your role.

## What To Check

- Is the task owner correct?
- Is the gig assigned to the right person?
- Is the correct agency selected?
- Is the calendar scope set to My Schedule or a broader view?
- Is the task already completed?
