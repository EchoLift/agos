## Check The Email

Workspace access is connected to your login email. Make sure you logged in with the same email that was invited.

## Check The Invitation

Ask the owner or manager to confirm the invitation is active and was sent to the correct email.

## Check Workspace Switcher

Open the profile menu and look under Switch Workspace.
