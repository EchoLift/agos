## Why It Happens

AGENCIE only allows actions when your current agency role has permission.

Examples:

- Writers cannot manage campaign lifecycle.
- Editors cannot change agency settings.
- Basic members cannot remove other members.

## What To Do

Ask an owner or manager to change your role if you genuinely need access.

> [!WARNING] Security
> Hiding a button is not the only protection. The backend also checks permissions.
