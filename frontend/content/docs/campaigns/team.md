## What It Is

Campaign team shows who is attached to a campaign: campaign manager, relationship manager, writers, editors, DOPs, designers, and social media manager.

## Visibility

Everyone can use team details as context. Managing the team is limited to authorized roles.

## Why It Matters

Creative employees need to know who to coordinate with, even when they cannot edit the campaign.

## What Happens Next

Adding someone to a campaign team gives context. Actual task ownership still depends on workflow tasks or gigs.
