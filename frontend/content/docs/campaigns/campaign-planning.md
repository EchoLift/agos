## When To Use Campaigns

Use campaigns for structured client work such as monthly retainers, product launches, festival plans, or weekly content commitments.

## What A Campaign Contains

- Client
- Campaign type
- Priority
- Dates
- Goal and KPI
- Key message
- Deliverables
- Team
- Publishing schedule
- References

## What Happens Next

Campaigns connect business planning to production. Publishing slots can generate content assets and workflow tasks.

> [!TIP] Use Gigs For Small Requests
> If the work is only one direct assignment, a gig is faster than a campaign.
