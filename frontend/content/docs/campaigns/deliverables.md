## What Deliverables Mean

Deliverables describe the promise to the client: for example, 8 reels, 4 static posts, or 12 stories.

## How To Use Them

Add the content type, quantity, frequency, posting days, time, and platform where available.

## Why They Matter

Deliverables help the owner and manager translate a client promise into publishing slots and production work.

## Limitation

Advanced recurring automation is still partial. You may need to adjust generated slots manually.
