## What It Does

Generate production creates the actual work needed to fulfill a publishing slot.

## Flow

Publishing slot creates content. Content creates workflow. Workflow creates the first production task.

## Example

A slot says Instagram Reel on Friday at 7 PM. Generate production creates a reel content item and starts the workflow so the writer, DOP, and editor can move it forward.

## What To Check

Make sure the campaign team and deadlines make sense before depending on the generated work.
