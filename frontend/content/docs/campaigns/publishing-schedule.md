## What It Is

Publishing schedule is the calendar of promised posts for a campaign.

Each slot can have a platform, time, status, linked content, notes, and publishing result.

## Common States

- Planned
- Ready
- Scheduled
- Published
- Missed
- Cancelled

## What Happens Next

When a slot is linked to content, AGENCIE can show whether the content is ready, at risk, missed, or published.

## Mark Published

When content goes live, mark the slot as published and add the published URL when available.
