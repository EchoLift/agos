## What It Solves

Client setup gives your team enough context to create content without asking the founder the same questions again.

## How To Create

1. Open Clients.
2. Choose Create Client.
3. Fill the required basics.
4. Add brand, audience, content goals, approval settings, and notes as needed.
5. Save.

## What Happens Next

The client becomes available in campaigns, gigs, content assets, and workflow context.

## Keep It Lightweight

Do not fill every field on day one. Add enough context to start work, then complete the playbook over time.
