## Current Status

AGOS has client-related role modeling, but a full client portal is not ready for daily use.

## What Clients Should Eventually See

Clients should only see approved deliverables, pending approvals, comments meant for them, and publishing status.

## What Clients Should Not See

Clients should not see internal team notes, employee disputes, incomplete internal stages, or agency-only planning details.

> [!NOT_AVAILABLE] Not Available Yet
> Do not onboard external client users as if a complete client portal exists today.
