## What It Is

The client playbook is the single place for brand voice, audience, business description, social platforms, goals, and working context.

## Who Sees What

Owners and managers see more operational context. Production roles see the client vibe they need for creative work.

## Useful Fields

- Brand voice
- Target audience
- Business description
- Pain points
- Content goals
- Social platforms
- Internal notes for agency users

## Why It Matters

A writer or editor should understand the client before creating work, without reading old WhatsApp chats.
