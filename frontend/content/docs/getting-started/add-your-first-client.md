## What This Is

A client in AGENCIE is more than a name. It is a playbook for the creative team: audience, brand voice, goals, social links, approval preferences, and internal notes.

## Fastest Flow

1. Open Clients.
2. Choose Create Client.
3. Fill required fields first.
4. Add brand voice, target audience, and strategy.
5. Save the client.

## What Happens Next

The client becomes available when creating campaigns, content, and gigs.

Employees can see the safe client context they need for creative work. Sensitive internal details should remain hidden from basic production roles.

## What To Fill First

At minimum, add client name, industry, target audience, brand voice, and content goals. You can enrich the playbook later.
