## Current Status

AGOS stores in-app notification records for selected workflow and assignment events.

## What Is Partial

A full notification center with unread counts, mark-read actions, and preferences is not complete in the current product UI.

## Planned Later

- Email delivery
- WhatsApp delivery
- Notification preferences
- Full read/unread inbox

> [!WARNING] Do Not Depend On Notifications Alone Yet
> For now, use My Work, Workflow, and Calendar as the dependable daily views.
