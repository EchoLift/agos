## Current Status

AGENCIE stores in-app notification records for selected workflow and assignment events. Email is reserved for operational moments where someone now needs to act, such as invitations, direct work assignment, review requests, requested changes, and actionable workflow handoffs.

AGENCIE's notification rule is:

> Visibility does not deserve an email. Responsibility does.

Campaign creation, campaign team assignment, generic workflow visibility, and minor metadata changes should remain in the workspace and activity log.

## What Is Partial

A full notification center with unread counts, mark-read actions, preferences, and complete Calendar-channel orchestration is not complete in the current product UI.

## Planned Later

- WhatsApp delivery
- Notification preferences
- Full read/unread inbox
- Unified action-required dispatch across in-app, email, and Google Calendar

> [!WARNING] Do Not Depend On Notifications Alone Yet
> For now, use My Work, Workflow, and Calendar as the dependable daily views.
