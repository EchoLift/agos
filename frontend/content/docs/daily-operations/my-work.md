## What It Is

My Work shows work assigned to you. It combines campaign tasks and gigs in one place.

## Who Uses It

Writers, DOPs, editors, designers, social media managers, and managers use My Work to know what needs attention today.

## What You See

- Assigned work
- Due today
- Waiting review
- Returned or overdue work
- Recent movement connected to your work

## What Happens Next

Open a task from My Work, complete the available action, and AGENCIE moves the item forward.

If your My Work is empty but you expected tasks, check whether the current task owner is actually you, not just someone with the same role.
