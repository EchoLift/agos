## What It Is

Calendar shows assigned work like meeting invites. It includes workflow deadlines, publishing slots, and gig due dates.

## Scopes

- **My Schedule** shows work directly assigned to you.
- **My Role** shows work for your role.
- **My Team** is for managers and owners.
- **Campaign** filters one campaign.
- **Agency** is for owners and managers.

## Visible Calendars

You can hide or show groups such as My Tasks, Publishing, Shoots, Reviews, and Approvals.

## What Can Go Wrong

If Calendar and My Work disagree, check whether the item is directly assigned to you or only assigned to someone with the same role.
