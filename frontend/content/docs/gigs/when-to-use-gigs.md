## What A Gig Is

A Gig is a direct work assignment with a clear requirement, assignee, and deadline.

It does not require a Campaign, campaign team, publishing schedule, or full content-production workflow.

Use a Gig when the work simply needs to be assigned, completed, submitted, and reviewed.

## Good Gig Examples

- Write 5 scripts by Friday
- Edit 3 urgent reels
- Create 12 thumbnails this week
- Write captions for a client
- Design a one-time poster
- Shoot product photos
- Prepare copy for an upcoming announcement

A Gig can be linked to a business client when the assignment belongs to that client.

## When To Use A Gig

A Gig works best when:

- The requirement is clear
- One person can own the assignment
- The work has a specific deadline
- A full campaign plan is unnecessary
- The result can be submitted directly for review

Think of a Gig as:

Requirement
→ Assignee
→ Work
→ Submission
→ Review
→ Completion

## When Not To Use A Gig

Do not use a Gig when the work belongs to a structured campaign with multiple deliverables, production stages, team responsibilities, and publishing schedules.

For example:

**Write 5 scripts by Friday**
→ Gig

**Produce 8 reels and 12 posts this month with writing, shooting, editing, reviews, and scheduled publishing**
→ Campaign

Campaigns are designed to coordinate larger client commitments across planning and production.

## Gig vs Workflow Task

A Gig is a standalone assignment.

A Workflow Task is part of a larger content-production process.

For example:

**Edit these 3 existing reels**
→ Gig

**Edit this reel after its shoot stage is completed**
→ Workflow Task

Use the structure that matches how the work actually needs to move.

## What Happens Next

After a Gig is created, the assignee can see the work in relevant operational views such as **My Work** and **Calendar**.

The assignee completes the work and submits notes, an external link, or both.

Where review is configured, the reviewer can then approve the work or request changes.

> [!TIP] Use The Smallest Useful Workflow
> If an assignment only needs an owner, deadline, submission, and review, use a Gig. Do not create a Campaign just to make five scripts feel administratively important.
