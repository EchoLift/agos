## What A Gig Is

A gig is a direct work assignment. It does not require a campaign or campaign team.

## Good Gig Examples

- Need 5 scripts by Friday
- Edit 3 urgent reels
- Create 12 thumbnails this week
- Write captions for a client

## When Not To Use A Gig

Do not use a gig for a structured monthly campaign with publishing schedules and multiple craft stages. Use Campaigns for that.

## What Happens Next

The assignee sees the gig in My Work and Calendar. The reviewer can approve or request changes.
